ABOUT FITNESS WITH DIAMOND

YOUR GUIDE TO
finding your fit in Dallas.

Fitness With Diamond is a weekly guide to fitness, wellness, and community events happening around Dallas.

Whether you're looking for a new run club, a Pilates class, a wellness experience, a social workout, or a fun way to get active without spending a fortune, this is where you'll find it.

Each week, I search for events worth knowing about and bring them together in one place — so you can spend less time searching and more time moving, connecting, and enjoying your city.

Fitness With Diamond is about more than working out. It's about finding your people, trying something new, and creating a lifestyle that feels good.

— Diamond ♡
